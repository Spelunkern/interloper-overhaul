## Cannery Manufacturing

The manufacturing features were reimplemented for Interloper Overhaul using the
current game APIs, with revised recipes and integration with its loot policies.
The feature set, efficient-arrow-shaft yield, and milling costs/durations were
informed by Cannery Manufacturing by ds5678 and the DLC update by Digitalzombie.
No upstream DLLs, settings, item bundles, or spawn packs are distributed here.
